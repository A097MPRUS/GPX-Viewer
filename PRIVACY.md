# Privacy

Author: A097MPRUS.

- **Your files stay on your Mac.** Opening, analysing, editing and exporting GPX files happens locally. Opening a file never changes it; edits are written only when you save.
- **No account, no analytics, no tracking.** The apps contain no analytics or advertising code and send no usage data.
- **What goes online, and when:**
  - **Map tiles:** when a map is shown, the apps request the tiles for the visible area from the selected map service. The service sees which area you are looking at, as with any online map.
  - **Search and addresses:** when you search for a place or ask for an address, that text or point goes to Geoportail Luxembourg or Apple Maps.
  - **Terrain:** when you load official terrain, the route's coordinates go to Geoportail Luxembourg (Luxembourg), IGN (France) or the AWS terrain tiles (elsewhere).
  - **Route analysis:** only when you choose "Analyze surface and route context", the route's coordinates go to Geoportail Luxembourg and to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service.
  - **Route planning:** when you draw a route with routing, the points go to BRouter.
  - **Commercial maps (both Mac apps, only with your own key):** when you show a Microsoft Azure Maps, TomTom, HERE or Mapbox map or layer (including live traffic and weather), the tiles for the visible area are requested from that company with your key, and for Azure Maps and HERE the visible area is also sent to get the credit line. Each company receives that under its own privacy terms and counts the requests against your account.
- **Keys and sign-in:** API keys you enter are stored in the macOS Keychain. Geoportail sign-in happens on Geoportail's own page; the app keeps only the session cookie and sends it only to Geoportail.
- **Location:** the apps never ask for or use your current location.
