It is not a navigation device and must not be relied on for navigation, route finding in the field, safety decisions or emergencies. Maps, routes, elevations, gradients, surfaces, distances, times and every other value it shows can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Routes it plans or edits may cross private land, closed roads, dangerous terrain or places where access is not allowed. ## 4. Your files and your privacy

GPX Viewer has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your files are opened, analysed and saved on your own computer. Some features send data to online services, but only when you use them:

- map tiles for the area you are looking at;
- place searches and addresses;
- the coordinates of a route when you ask for official terrain, route analysis or route planning.

Each service receives that data under its own privacy terms. The details are in PRIVACY.md. You are responsible for the files you open, edit and share, including any personal or location data in them, and for having the right to use them.
