## Move and zoom

Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom. On a Mac, the compass and zoom buttons stay in the lower right corner, clear of the map credits.

## Maps and Layers

Open Maps and Layers from the toolbar:

- **Base maps:** Apple Maps (Mac), OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, CyclOSM, official aerial and topographic maps, and the best satellite or hybrid mosaic, which prefers official imagery by country.
- **Overlays:** cycle infrastructure, hiking, MTB and signposted cycle routes, roads and place names. Each overlay has its own opacity.
- **Keyed maps:** Thunderforest, Esri, and on Mac Microsoft Azure Maps, TomTom, HERE and Mapbox, with your own API key.

When your route lies where official imagery exists, GPX Viewer offers it in a banner.

## Credits

Every map shows its owner's credit. On narrow windows the credit line shortens; the info button lists the full credits and terms.

## Map and graph together

Hovering the route within a few points moves the graph cursor to that position; moving along the graph moves the marker on the map. Selecting a section on the graph fits the map to that section.

## Places

Find a place searches with Geoportail Luxembourg or Apple Maps. See [Privacy and data handling](../privacy-data/) for what is sent.
